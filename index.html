02 — about me

I build where hardware meets software, and I like leading the people who make it happen.

Hi, I'm Havish Pydimarri, a Computer Engineering student at Iowa State University. I started my engineering journey studying Computer Science at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus, and I'm now building on that foundation in Ames.

My interests sit at two ends of the stack. On one side, I'm drawn to Large Language Models, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, backend engineering and designing systems that scale. On the other, this semester I'm programming the CyBot, an autonomous robot, in C for Embedded Systems (CPRE 288), and learning how circuits actually behave in EE 2300.

Outside the lab, I've spent a lot of time leading. I represented 700+ students as a hostel representative, secured sponsorships for tech expos and overnight hackathons, and ran logistics for a campus technical festival. Those roles taught me that good engineering is as much about people as it is about code.

I'm looking for a Summer 2027 internship where I can build real systems and keep learning fast.
